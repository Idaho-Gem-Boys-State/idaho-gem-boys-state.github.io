## Who can attend

Gem Boys State is for high school **juniors**, the students who will be seniors next fall. Juniors from **public, private, parochial, and home schools** are all eligible.

Looking for the program for young women? Visit [Idaho Syringa Girls State]({{ site.links.girls_state }}).

## How to get selected

{% steps %}
1. **Find an American Legion Post near you.** Use the Legion's [Find a Post directory]({{ site.links.find_a_post }}). Tip: search near your town, then check **Boys State** under "Post Program" to narrow the list.
2. **Contact the Post.** Ask whether they're sponsoring delegates for Gem Boys State {{ site.session.year }}, how they choose them, and what they need from you.
3. **Get selected.** The Post selects its delegates and registers them with Gem Boys State. The cost is normally paid by the Post, a local business, or a community group.
4. **Get ready for the week.** Once you're registered, [Getting Ready](/getting-ready/) covers what to bring and what to wear.
{% endsteps %}

{% callout "Start early", "gold" %}
Spots are limited to {{ site.session.capacity }} delegates. Once they fill, a stand-by list is used. {{ site.session.deadline_sentence }} Posts need time to select and register delegates before then, so reach out early.
{% endcallout %}

**Having trouble reaching a Post?** Email us at [{{ site.contact.email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact.email }}).

## What it costs

The cost is **{{ site.cost.text }} per delegate**. In most cases, it's paid by the sponsoring American Legion Post, a local business, or a community group.

Uniform shirts and three meals a day are provided. Delegates bring money for lunch on the two days at the Idaho State Capitol.

## For school counselors and teachers

You know which juniors are ready for a challenge. Here's how to help one get to Gem Boys State:

- **Share this page.** It's the quickest explanation of how selection works.
- **Connect students with a Post** using the [Find a Post directory]({{ site.links.find_a_post }}).
- **Encourage them to start early.** Posts need time to select and register delegates before the deadline.
- **Print it.** This page prints cleanly as a handout.

## Questions about applying

{% faq "Can I apply directly to Gem Boys State?" %}
No. Every delegate is selected and registered by a local American Legion Post. Start by [finding a Post near you]({{ site.links.find_a_post }}).
{% endfaq %}

{% faq "Do I have to go to a public school?" %}
No. Juniors from public, private, parochial, and home schools are all eligible.
{% endfaq %}

{% faq "What happens if the session is full?" %}
After {{ site.session.capacity }} delegates are registered, a stand-by list is used. Contact your local Post about the stand-by list.
{% endfaq %}

{% faq "Does my family have to pay?" %}
The {{ site.cost.text }} cost is normally paid by the sponsoring Post, a local business, or a community group. Ask your Post how it handles sponsorship. Delegates should bring money for lunch on the two days at the Capitol.
{% endfaq %}

{% faq "Is there a program for young women?" %}
Yes. Visit [Idaho Syringa Girls State]({{ site.links.girls_state }}).
