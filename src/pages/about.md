## What is Boys State?

Boys State has been a program of The American Legion since 1935. Sessions are held across the country, and Gem Boys State is Idaho's.

During the week, delegates run a simulated state government at the city, county, and state levels. They form political parties, campaign, hold elections, write and debate legislation, take part in courts, hear from high-caliber speakers, and spend the final two days at the Idaho State Capitol.

## Who runs it

Gem Boys State is a program of The American Legion, Department of Idaho. Delegates are selected and sponsored by local American Legion Posts across the state.

The week is run by volunteer counselors and deans, all of them former delegates, alongside members of The American Legion. [Meet the staff](/staff/).
