## Why we keep it a surprise

Part of what makes the week memorable is not knowing what's coming next, so we describe it only in broad strokes. You'll find out the rest when you get there.

**Parents:** we know a surprise is a lot to ask. [Getting Ready](/getting-ready/) covers everything you need to plan: what to bring, the dress code, and logistics.

{% photo "", "delegates during the week" %}

## What you'll take home

- **Leadership.** Find out what kind of leader you are when the responsibility is real.
- **Public speaking.** Get comfortable making your case in front of a room.
- **Friendships.** Meet motivated juniors from across Idaho.
- **An understanding of how government works,** because you ran it.
- **A shot at Boys Nation.** At the end of the week, two delegates are chosen to represent Idaho at [American Legion Boys Nation](/boys-nation/) in Washington, D.C.
