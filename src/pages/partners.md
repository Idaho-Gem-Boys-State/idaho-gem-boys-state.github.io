## Sponsor a delegate

It costs {{ site.cost.text }} to send one delegate to Gem Boys State. That cost is normally paid by the sponsoring American Legion Post, a local business, or a community group.

To sponsor a delegate from your community:

{% steps %}
1. **Find a local Post** using the [Find a Post directory]({{ site.links.find_a_post }}).
2. **Offer to sponsor** one or more delegates from your area.
3. **The Post handles the rest,** selecting and registering its delegates.
{% endsteps %}
