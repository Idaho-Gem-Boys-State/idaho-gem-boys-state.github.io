## What to bring

- **Bedding** for a bunk bed
- **Toiletries**
- **Slacks or khakis** for daytime
- **Closed-toe shoes**
- **Athletic clothes**
- **A phone** (and its charger)
- **Money for lunch** on the two days at the Idaho State Capitol

{% callout "Provided for you" %}
- **Uniform shirts**
- **Three meals a day**, except lunch on the two days at the Capitol
{% endcallout %}

## Dress code

- **During the day:** slacks or khakis and closed-toe shoes.
- **Not allowed during the day:** denim, shorts, or hats.
- **At night:** regular clothes and pajamas are fine.
- **No suit** needed.
