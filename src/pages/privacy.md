## What this site collects

- **No forms, accounts, or sign-ups.** This site never asks for student information.
- **No advertising or tracking cookies.**
- **Visitor counts only.** The site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal information.
