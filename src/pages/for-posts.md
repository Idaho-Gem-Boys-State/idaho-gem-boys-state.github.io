## Registering a delegate

{% steps %}
1. **Select your delegates.** Choose high school juniors (rising seniors) from public, private, parochial, or home schools. Use the application packet to select your candidates.
2. **Mail the paperwork.** For each delegate, send the **signed application pages**, a **copy of the delegate's birth certificate**, and a **check for {{ site.cost.text }}** to the Department office (address below).
3. **Email the delegate's contact details** to [{{ site.registration.email }}](mailto:{{ site.registration.email }}). The list of details is below.
4. **Watch the deadline.** {{ site.session.deadline_sentence }} Spots are limited to {{ site.session.capacity }} delegates; after that, a stand-by list is used, so send selections in as soon as you can.
{% endsteps %}

### What to email for each delegate

Send these to [{{ site.registration.email }}](mailto:{{ site.registration.email }}):

- Delegate's name, email address, and phone number
- Parent or guardian's name, email address, and phone number
- Your Post number
- Name, email address, and phone number of your Post Commander or Boys State Chairman

## Finding a sponsor

If your Post can't cover the full cost, a local business or community group can sponsor a delegate from your area. Point them to our [Partners & Supporters](/partners/) page.

## Spread the word

Help local juniors find out about Gem Boys State. Share our [How to Attend](/how-to-attend/) page with high school counselors, teachers, and families in your area. It explains the program and how Post selection works, and it prints cleanly as a handout.
